Citing Concordant Modes
=======================

If you use CMA in published work, please cite the papers describing the
method:

.. [CMA2022] M. E. Lahm, N. L. Kitzmiller, H. F. Mull, W. D. Allen and
   H. F. Schaefer III, "Concordant Mode Approach for Molecular Vibrations",
   *J. Am. Chem. Soc.* **2022**, *144*, 23271–23274.
   https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.2c11158

.. [CMA2024] N. L. Kitzmiller, M. E. Lahm, L. N. Olive Dornshuld, J. Jin,
   W. D. Allen and H. F. Schaefer III, "Convergent Concordant Mode Approach
   for Molecular Vibrations: CMA-2", *J. Chem. Theory Comput.* **2024**,
   *20*, 10886–10898. https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.4c01240

.. [CMA2026] L. N. Olive Dornshuld *et al.*, "Concordant Mode Approach (CMA):
   Vibrational Analysis of New and Upgraded Intermolecular Benchmarks for
   Noncovalent Bonding", *J. Phys. Chem. A* **2026**, *130*, 3249–3260.
   https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.6c00689

The program itself is available at https://github.com/CCQC/ConcordantModes.
