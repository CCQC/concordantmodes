Methanol in delocalized coordinates
===================================

For a larger molecule, writing a good set of internal coordinates by hand is
tedious. With ``coords = "Delocalized"`` it is enough to give the bonds; CMA
generates all angles, torsions and out-of-plane coordinates and removes the
redundancies (:doc:`../theory/index`). This follows
``examples/2_Methanol/molpro/CMA0A/standard``.

.. literalinclude:: ../../../examples/2_Methanol/molpro/CMA0A/standard/zmat
   :caption: zmat (five bonds)

Instead of listing bonds you can set ``covalent_radii = true`` and leave out
the ZMAT block; atoms closer than ``bond_threshold`` times the sum of their
covalent radii are bonded.

The templates in this example place the geometry with a line number
(``cart_insert_a = 9``) instead of a ``{geometry}`` line, which older inputs
use; either works:

.. code-block:: toml
   :caption: cma.toml

   coords = "Delocalized"
   program_a = "molpro"
   program_b = "molpro"
   cart_insert_a = 9
   cart_insert_b = 9

Methanol has :math:`3\cdot 6 - 6 = 12` vibrational coordinates:

.. code-block:: console

   $ cma run
   Level B: wrote 157 molpro inputs to DispsB/

A full Hessian needs :math:`1 + 12\cdot 13 = 157` energies, while CMA-0A needs
only :math:`1 + 2\cdot 12 = 25` at level A. With level B a cheaper method than
level A (the example uses CCSD(T) for both, with a smaller basis at level B),
the saving grows with the size of the molecule.

The total energy distribution printed at the end shows which internal
coordinates make up each mode. With delocalized coordinates many of these are
combinations generated by CMA; ``verbosity = "debug"`` lists them.
