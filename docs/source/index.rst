Concordant Modes
================

Concordant Modes (CMA) computes harmonic vibrational frequencies at a high
level of theory for a fraction of the usual cost. A cheap level of theory
(level B) gives the normal modes; the expensive method (level A) is then only
used for displacements along those modes. The method is described in
*J. Am. Chem. Soc.* 2022, **144**, 23271–23274; *J. Chem. Theory Comput.*
2024, **20**, 10886–10898; and *J. Phys. Chem. A* 2026, **130**, 3249–3260.
